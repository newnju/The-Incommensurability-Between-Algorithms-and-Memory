## Decision rule (preregistered)

- All four checks pass → image may be published at full size.
- Redistribution restricted → thumbnail-only (≤512 px, non-print) in this
  repository; exclusion from the original-resolution image archive.
- Redistribution prohibited → excluded from all public deposits; noted in the
  coding table (presence/absence counts remain reportable).

## Findings

For all six platforms (M1–M6): the conditions in force at generation time
(2026-08/09) place no restriction on academic use, academic publication, or
scholarly redistribution of generated images. Under the preregistered decision
rule, all four checks pass and all images are published at full size.

## Training-set images

The 18 training-set images (6 models × 3 prompt conditions, one image per cell)
were generated by the six studied models (M1–M6) for coder calibration, and are
included in `images/训练集/` (directory structure documented in
`codebook/README.md`). They are excluded from the formal analysis: 18 units × 10
shots = 180 images generated, 1 training image discarded per unit, leaving 162
images coded.
